# Privacy Policy - PondPulse

_Last updated: July 2026_

PondPulse does **not collect, store, or share any personal data**.

The app runs entirely offline. Your game progress - which levels you've solved, your
star ratings, your Splash Rush best scores, your hints, your chosen theme, pond friend,
lily pad style and language - is saved **only on your own device** using the system's
standard local storage, and is never transmitted anywhere.

- We do **not** use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
- We do **not** use third-party services or SDKs, except the store's own billing service
  (see "In-app purchases" below).
- We do **not** request any device permissions.
- We do **not** have user accounts or sign-in.

**In-app purchases:** PondPulse offers optional one-time purchases (for example, the
Premium upgrade, hint packs, or cosmetic themes). Payments are processed entirely by the
**App Store** (on iOS) or **Google Play** (on Android). We never see, receive, or store
your payment details. The store only tells the app which products you own so it can
unlock them; that ownership record is managed by the store and cached on your device.

Because the app collects no data, there is nothing for us to access, share, delete, or
export. Deleting the app removes the locally stored progress from your device. Purchases
can be restored on a new device through the store ("Restore Purchases" in the Pond Shop).

**Children's privacy:** PondPulse is suitable for all ages and collects no data from
anyone, including children.
