## How to play

- Tap open water to make a splash.
- Every floater drifts one step away from your splash — always.
- Banks, rocks and other floaters block the drift; current arrows carry floaters one extra step after every splash.
- Bring every duckling to a lily pad before the splashes run out. Colored ducklings need a pad of their own color.
- Stuck? Tap **Hint** — the built-in solver glows the next move of a shortest solution.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does the game work offline?**
Yes. PondPulse is fully offline — no internet connection is required.

**Are there ads or sign-ups?**
No ads and no sign-up, ever. The first 300 levels are free; an optional one-time
**Premium** purchase ($2.99) unlocks stages 21–30 (150 more levels), opens every level
in any order, grants unlimited hints and includes all exclusive cosmetics and future
packs. Small one-time purchases can also unlock individual themes, pond friends and
lily pad styles, and a 50-hint pack.

**I bought Premium on a new device — where is it?**
Open the **Pond Shop** and tap **Restore Purchases** at the bottom. Purchases are tied
to your App Store account, not to the device.

**I ran out of hints.**
Each level only ever spends one hint — showing its hint again later is free. You can buy
50 more hints in the shop, or unlock Premium for unlimited hints.

**Does resetting progress remove my purchases?**
No. Reset (Settings → Reset progress) clears stars and Splash Rush scores only.
Purchases and cosmetics always survive.

**How are the star ratings decided?**
Solve within the level's par for 3 stars, one splash over par for 2, anything else 1.
Every level's par is proven optimal by the same solver that powers hints.

**Can I change the language?**
Yes — Settings → Language offers 16 languages, applied instantly. Hebrew and Arabic
flip the whole app right-to-left.
